Our Mission

Career Tech High Academy is a public charter high school in Hågat Bay, Guam, dedicated to:

  • Building a community-centered identity grounded in our island's heritage,
  • Developing a skilled workforce ready for the careers of tomorrow, and
  • Serving as a forum where young leaders grow and give back.

Need a sign language interpreter?

Deaf and hard-of-hearing students, families, and visitors can use the call button in the corner of this page any time to connect with a live video sign language interpreter — and bring school staff, a doctor's office, or emergency services into the same call. No appointment needed.
